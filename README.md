<h2>👨‍💻 About Me</h2>

I'm a **Software Engineer** with over **3 years of professional experience**, currently specializing in building **AI Agents, AI Automation Systems, and Browser Automation Pipelines**, backed by a strong foundation in **Full Stack Web Development** (MERN Stack & Laravel).

I design and build intelligent, autonomous systems that **replace manual workflows** — from AI Sales Agents that qualify and follow up with leads, to browser-automation agents that scrape, fill forms, and interact with websites like a human would.

My core focus areas right now:
- 🤖 **AI Agents** – Autonomous, tool-using agents built with LangChain, LangGraph & CrewAI
- 🧲 **AI Sales & Outreach Agents** – Lead qualification, personalized cold outreach, follow-up automation
- 🕸️ **Browser Automation** – Puppeteer & Playwright-based agents for scraping, form-filling, and web interaction
- ⚙️ **AI Workflow Automation** – End-to-end automated pipelines connecting APIs, LLMs, and business tools
- 📚 **RAG Systems** – Retrieval-Augmented Generation using vector databases for context-aware AI responses

I also bring **2+ years of full-stack development experience** (React.js, Next.js, Node.js, Laravel) to build production-ready, scalable interfaces and backends around these AI systems — not just prototypes.
